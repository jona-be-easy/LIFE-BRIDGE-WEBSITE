From end-stage renal failure requiring taxing dialysis, to severe liver failure and corneal blindness, many vibrant lives are cut short simply because the right donor was never found. Organ donation is not merely a medical procedure—it is an extraordinary legacy of empathy that restores entire families, community livelihoods, and futures.

Safe, Ethical, and Transparent Donation

Choosing to become an organ donor is a decision grounded in trust. We adhere strictly to ethical guidelines and health standards set by national medical authorities. Every step—from voluntary registration to final surgical procedure—is handled with the highest level of respect, care, and medical privacy for donors and their families.

Connecting Lives Through Organ Donation

Our mission is to eliminate preventable loss of life across Uganda by establishing a reliable network of willing donors, providing timely emergency coordination, and offering compassionate support to patients in urgent need of organ and tissue transplants.
