Every life saved
begins with a bridge

LIFE BRIDGE connects people who choose to give with people who are waiting for an organ transplant through licensed transplant centers, fair matching and consent at every step.

In Uganda, thousands suffer from kidney failure, liver failure and blindness due to cornea disease. Without donors, many die waiting. WE BRIDGE THAT GAP.


Connecting Lives Through Organ Donation

Our mission is to help bridge the gap between willing organ donors and people who need transplants - providing timely services to those in need of organs.
