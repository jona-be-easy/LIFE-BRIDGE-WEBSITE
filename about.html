ABOUT LIFE BRIDGE

Built so that no one waits for want of a connection.

Life Bridge is a [NON-PROFIT / SOCIAL ENTERPRISE] that helps people who want to donate and people who need a transplant find the right path, safely and fairly.

We work alongside licensed transplant centers, ethics committees and donor families. We do not make medical decisions or allocate organs. We make the process clearer, kinder and easier to trust.

Life bridge began with painful fact: people are dying while organs that could have saved them are out of reach mainly due too few donors or limited time to transport the existing organs.

It felt wrong that the gap between a donor and a waiting patient could be decided by a delay, a missed call or a road that took too long and so we set out to close that gap.

We started by making it easier for people to say yes to donation, and for their families to know and honor that choice. We then worked with transplant centers and transport partners so that when an organ is available, it moves quickly and safely to the person who is waiting. We are not doctors, and we don't decide who receives an organ. Clinicians do that, under fair rules.

Behind every number on this page is a donor who chose to give, a family who said yes in their hardest hour, and someone who woke up to more time. That is who Life Bridge is built for.

  • Our mission

    To connect willing donors with patients in need through a process people can trust.

  • Our approach

    Consent first, medicine led by clinicians, and every decision explained in plain language.

  • Our promise

    No payment for organs, no shortcuts and no one treated as a statistic.
